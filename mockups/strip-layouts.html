Scribe strip: layouts

A is the docked strip now in the game. Everything after it is a box that appears when something is clicked, so it floats over the scene (which keeps one size) and goes away again. Each frame has a button to show or hide its box, so you can see the screen both ways. There is no task list: the scene shows what is still wrong. Hint always sits with Journal, never with Restart and Levels. The type buttons below change every frame except the minigame ones. The last part is a sandbox for trying any mix.

Part 1 · Box formats

Three ways to dress the box. A is the docked strip for comparison. B to D all have Journal and Hint in the top bar.

Part 2 · Where Journal and Hint go

The box is not always there, so Journal and Hint cannot live in it. All three use the same narrow box. Use the show and hide button to check the screen without it.

Part 3 · Minigame screens

A minigame fills the screen under the top bar, so the playfield is framed by the band all round and the box sits over it. The playfield is a placeholder. The box is the deep-papyrus band box, as on the scene screens.

Part 4 · Sandbox

Every switch applies to the frame below.
